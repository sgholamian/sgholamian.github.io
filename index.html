About

I work on getting AI systems out of the demo stage and into production, in domains where being wrong is expensive. I lead a team of 10+ researchers and engineers at Thomson Reuters AI Labs, where we have taken agentic AI from research to enterprise scale across tax, audit, legal, and commerce.

Most of my time goes to the unglamorous half of that: evaluation that catches regressions before customers do, domain adaptation of foundation models, and the orchestration and monitoring that make an agent something you can actually operate. Behind it are 10+ years of machine learning research across industry and a Ph.D., and before that, chipset architecture at Qualcomm and Intel and on-device machine learning for wearables at North Inc. (acquired by Google).

Advisory services. I take on a small number of advisory engagements with early-stage AI teams, typically a few hours a week. What I advise on.
